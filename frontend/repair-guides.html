Repair safety

Know when to stop and get professional help.

⚠️ Battery and heat warning Never puncture, crush, bend, or pry aggressively against a lithium-ion battery. Do not heat a swollen, leaking, smoking, or damaged battery. Stop work if you notice unusual heat, smell, smoke, or swelling.
• Avoid metal tools near exposed batteries and connectors.
• Do not continue if the battery appears damaged.
• Follow the exact model's service manual.
• Seek a qualified technician for high-risk repairs.
